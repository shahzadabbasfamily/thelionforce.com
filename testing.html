Questions creators ask us.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Who can join The Lion Force?

Creators aged 18 or over with an account in good standing. There's no minimum follower count, though creators who already go LIVE regularly usually grow fastest with us.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining is free, and your manager explains exactly how the partnership works before you accept anything in the TikTok app.

Is The Lion Force an official TikTok agency?

Yes. We're part of TikTok's LIVE Creator Network, so you join us through an official invite inside the TikTok app, not through a third-party contract.

How quickly will I hear back?

Usually within 48 hours. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach a manager for your region.

How do I make a complaint or report a problem?

Lodge it directly in our creator CRM. You can follow its progress there, and your manager is notified as soon as you submit it. You can also message your manager on WhatsApp.

What happens if my account gets restricted?

Log it in our CRM or tell your manager straight away. We'll review what happened, explain the relevant policy and escalate eligible cases to TikTok through our agency channel. Final decisions are always TikTok's.

Can I leave if it's not right for me?

Yes. Talk to your manager and we'll explain how to leave the agency through TikTok's process.
