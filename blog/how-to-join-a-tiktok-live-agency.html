How to Join a TikTok LIVE Agency in 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

Quick answer

To join a TikTok LIVE agency: (1) make sure you can go LIVE (18+, LIVE available in your region, no active violations), (2) choose an official, TikTok-approved agency, (3) talk to one of its managers, (4) accept the agency's invite inside the TikTok app, and (5) plan your first LIVEs with your manager.

Before you start: what you need

An agency can only help you on LIVE if you can go LIVE in the first place. Check these first:

  • You are 18 or over. You must be 18+ to go LIVE and receive Gifts on TikTok.
  • LIVE works on your account. Tap + in the TikTok app and see whether the LIVE option appears. TikTok sets LIVE access rules, and they can differ by region and change over time, so your own app is the best guide.
  • Your account is in good standing. Active Community Guidelines violations or LIVE restrictions can block features. Tell the agency about any issues up front.
  • You are not tied to another agency. If you already work with one, sort out how leaving works before you accept a new invite.

How to join a TikTok LIVE agency: the 5 steps

  1. Check you can go LIVE. Confirm you are 18+, that LIVE appears in your app and that you have no active violations.
  2. Pick a real agency. Look for an official TikTok LIVE Creator Network agency with a registered company, named owners, real hosts you can find on TikTok, and no joining fee.
  3. Talk to a manager. Message the agency and ask the six questions below. A good agency answers clearly and doesn't rush you.
  4. Accept the invite inside TikTok. The agency sends you an official invitation, often as an invite link that opens in the TikTok app. Read it and tap accept yourself. Nobody else needs to touch your account.
  5. Plan your first LIVEs. Agree a schedule with your manager, get your onboarding and policy training, and join the agency's host community.

How the TikTok agency invite works

Joining happens inside the TikTok app. There is no form to fill in on another website and no login to hand over. In practice it works in one of two ways:

Option 1: the agency sends you an invite link

Many agencies, including The Lion Force, share an official TikTok invite link for each region. You open it on your phone, it opens in TikTok, and you review and accept the invitation there.

Option 2: you share your Agency Center code

The agency may ask for your invitation code so it can send you an invite. In many versions of the app it sits under Settings and privacy › Creator tools › LIVE Center › Agency Center. Once the agency sends the invitation, you read it and accept it in the app.

Good to know: TikTok sometimes renames or moves menus between app versions and regions. If you can't find the invite or code, update the app and ask your manager to walk you through it. They should never ask for your password to "do it for you".

Once you join, the agency can see performance data such as your Diamonds and LIVE hours. That's normal and it's how your manager coaches you, but it's another reason to choose an agency you trust.

Safe invite vs scam invite

Fake "agencies" target new creators. A real invite always looks like the left-hand column.

  • Password or login code requests. No real agency needs them. Joining is something you accept yourself.
  • Fees or coin top-ups. Official agencies are paid by TikTok, not by you.
  • Forms or links outside TikTok that ask for your account details. The official invite is accepted in the app.
  • Requests to forward Gift money. Your Gift earnings are yours.
  • Pressure to accept immediately. A genuine agency gives you time to decide.

Before you accept, ask for the agency's registered company name and look it up. In the UK you can do that for free on Companies House.

Why your region matters

TikTok approves agencies for specific regions, so an agency can only invite you if it covers the region your TikTok account belongs to. That is why the same agency may have different invite links for different countries, and why the first question a manager asks is usually "where is your account from?".

Common problems when joining (and fixes)

"I can't see the invitation."

Update TikTok, restart the app and open the invite link again on your phone, not on a computer. If it still doesn't show, your manager can check whether the invite was sent to the right account and region.

"The LIVE option doesn't appear."

LIVE access depends on TikTok's rules for your age, region and account status. An agency can't switch LIVE on for you, but a manager can help you understand why it's missing.

"I'm already in another agency."

Talk to your current agency about leaving first, and ask whether there is a waiting period before you can join a new one. Don't accept a new invite until that is clear.

"My account has a restriction."

Tell the agency before you join. Official agencies can escalate eligible cases to TikTok, but TikTok makes the final decision and nobody can honestly guarantee the outcome.

What to do in your first month

  • Week 1: meet your manager, agree a realistic LIVE schedule and do your policy training.
  • Week 2: go LIVE at consistent times, then review your numbers with your manager.
  • Week 3: try PK battles with other hosts in the agency and join a challenge.
  • Week 4: set your next goal together, such as LIVE hours, new followers or a Diamond milestone.

Consistency matters more than length. A steady schedule your viewers can remember usually beats random, very long streams.

Frequently asked questions

How do I join a TikTok LIVE agency?

Make sure you can go LIVE (18+, LIVE available in your region, no active violations), choose an official TikTok-approved agency, talk to one of its managers, then accept the agency's invitation inside the TikTok app. Joining a real agency is free and never requires your password.

Is there a fee to join a TikTok agency?

No. Official TikTok LIVE agencies are paid by TikTok, so they should never charge you a joining, training or verification fee. Asking for money is a red flag.

How many followers do I need to join a TikTok LIVE agency?

There is no single number that applies everywhere. TikTok sets the rules for going LIVE, and they can differ by region and change over time, so check whether the LIVE option appears in your app. Each agency also decides who it invites, so ask the manager directly.

Where do I find my TikTok Agency Center invitation code?

In many versions of the app it is under Settings and privacy, then Creator tools, then LIVE Center, then Agency Center. Menu names can change between app versions and regions, so if you can't find it, update the app and ask your agency manager to guide you.

Do I have to give an agency my TikTok password?

Never. A real agency sends you an invitation that you accept yourself in the TikTok app. Anyone asking for your password or login code is not a genuine agency.

Can I join a new agency if I'm already in one?

Sort out leaving with your current agency first and ask whether there is a waiting period before you can join another one. Don't accept a new invitation until that is clear.

Can I join a TikTok LIVE agency from Pakistan?

It depends on the region your TikTok account belongs to, because agencies are approved for specific regions, and LIVE features can differ by country.
